Hello, I'm Anan (or Ananthajith, Sanskrit for "infinite victories and happiness").

I am a Biomedical Sciences (Hons) undergraduate at the University of Edinburgh, transitioning into my final year with a predicted First Class Honours classification. I study the intersection of science and technology, applying computational and wet-lab approaches to understand complex biology.

Currently, I am co-leading the Edinburgh iGEM 2026 dry-lab team to build a synthetic biology biosensor. Previously, I optimized protein extraction in C. elegans and discovered a key coding subunit of the GBA gene linked to Gaucher-Parkinson's disease. I believe scientific discovery is driven by genuine curiosity, rigorous analysis, and a touch of creative expression.
